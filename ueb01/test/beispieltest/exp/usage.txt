Usage:

  ueb01 -h

    shows this help and exits.

  - or -

  ueb01 NUM1 OP NUM2

    computes a binary integer operation and prints the result. A call consists
    of a number followed by an operation and another number, where:

    NUM1 and NUM2 have to be positive integer numbers >= 1.

    OP is one of:
      d looks for the greatest common divisor of NUM1 and NUM2
      m looks for the least common multiple of NUM1 and NUM2
      p calculates the NUM2th power of NUM1
